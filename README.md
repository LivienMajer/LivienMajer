### Hey, I'm Livien 👋

CS master's student at **KIT** and research assistant at **Fraunhofer IOSB**.
I mostly work on getting machines to understand video, and lately on getting a humanoid robot to do something useful with that.

#### 🔬 What I'm working on
- 🤖 **Humanoid robotics:** VR teleoperation + MuJoCo / Isaac Sim setup for a Unitree G1, laying the groundwork for vision-language-action models
- 🎥 **Multi-modal video learning:** aligning RGB, IR, depth and 3D skeletons without labels
- 📐 **LLMs × Lean 4:** can a theorem prover keep learning without forgetting? (LoRA vs. retrieval)

#### 📌 Highlights
- 📄 [**Multi-modal Video Representation Alignment for Robust Self-supervised Driver Distraction Detection**](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.02352): IEEE ITSC 2026, presented it as an oral in Naples 🇮🇹 · [code](https://github.com/LivienMajer/Cross-Modal-Video-Representation-Alignment-CMVRA)
- 🧠 [**EEG-Mindreading**](https://github.com/LivienMajer/EEG-Mindreading): we tried to read minds at the National AI Hackathon 2026 and finished **2nd of 30 teams** (44.9 % top-1 EEG → video retrieval vs. 3.9 % baseline)
- 🦾 [**AidForge**](https://github.com/LivienMajer/aidforge): snap a photo of an everyday object and get a print-ready, custom-fit assistive device in about a minute. Gemini writes the CAD, and geometry checks make sure it actually prints. Built in one day at Tech Europe x Google DeepMind hackathon
- 🛠️ Plus a few side projects in my repos

#### 🧰 Usual toolbox
Python · PyTorch · Hugging Face · LoRA · MuJoCo · Isaac Sim · Lean 4 · Gemini API · OpenSCAD · SLURM
